When to check in with a doctor
  • You can't keep fluids down without throwing up
  • You've lost more than ~5% of your body weight, or weight keeps dropping past two weeks
  • Appetite hasn't improved at all by the end of week three
  • Low mood feels heavy or hard to shake

This is a sensitive stretch. If you're struggling more than feels manageable, reaching out to a doctor or someone you trust is a strong move, not a setback.
